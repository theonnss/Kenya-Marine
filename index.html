History of Kenya Marine Supplies

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd is a company involved in providing marine equipment and supplies to support maritime activities. These services help ships and other vessels operate safely and efficiently.

Marine Transportation

Marine transportation involves moving goods and passengers from one place to another using ships, boats and other water vessels. It plays an important role in international trade and the movement of cargo through the Port of Mombasa.

Marine Equipment and Supplies

Marine equipment includes ropes, anchors, navigation systems, safety jackets and communication devices. These supplies are essential for the safety and proper functioning of ships during their operations at sea.

Port Operations and Maritime Safety

Port operations involve loading and unloading cargo, storing goods and helping ships enter and leave the port. Maritime safety practices help protect crew members, passengers, cargo and the marine environment from accidents and pollution.
